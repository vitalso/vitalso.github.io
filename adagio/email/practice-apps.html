Use the Practice app to track student progress

Hi, Jessie Wheeler

I want to stay in sync with your students’ practice between lessons? Encourage them to download the Practice app a simple companion to Adagio that lets students track their practice like a fitness tracker. With the app, students can:

  • Log time and pieces practiced
  • Take notes and set personal to-dos
  • Stay on top of goals and assignments

When they join your class through the app, their practice data (session length, frequency, assigned goals) automatically appears in Adagio keeping you up-to-date without chasing info

How students join your class:

1. Via the app (Profile → Settings → Join a class → Search your name)

2. Via email invite (Add students in Adagio with their email and they’ll get an invite automatically)

Note: Students must accept your class invite for you to see their shared data. Thanks for helping students stay accountable and grow between lessons!
