Ready to get more out of Adagio? Start by adding your students as this is the key step that unlocks everything else.

Think of Adagio like a smart address book: once you add names, emails, and details for each student, you can track progress, assign goals, and connect seamlessly with the Practice app.

3 Easy Ways to Add Students:

1. One by one → Use the “Add Student” button in Adagio.

2. Bulk add → Paste a list of student emails to add them all at once.

3. Student search → Ask students to join your class via the Practice app: Profile > Settings > Join Class > Search your name.

Once students are added, they’ll appear in your dashboard and you’ll be able to send invites, assign goals, and view their practice data (once they join your class in the Practice app).
