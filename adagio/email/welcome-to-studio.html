Welcome to Adagio, How to Get Started

Welcome to Adagio the all-in-one app to manage your music studio or classroom.

With Adagio, you can:

  • 🎵 Track student practice time
  • 📆 Schedule lessons
  • 📝 Assign and review homework
  • 💬 Share notes and feedback
  • 💳 Automate billing and invoices

Step 1: Add Your Students
Everything in Adagio starts with your student list. Add students one-by-one, in bulk, or have them join you via the Practice App.

Their email enables messaging, scheduling, and practice tracking. Once added, students will get an invite to download the Practice app their main tool for logging practice and staying connected.

Next: Explore!
After setup, explore features like calendar scheduling, homework assignments, and your class feed to comment and encourage progress. You’re all set to make teaching easier and more connected.
